How this works. Live wind, wave height, sea temperature and UV come from Open-Meteo, fetched straight from your browser — no account, no tracking. Beach locations and amenities come from OpenStreetMap contributors.

Conditions are a forecast model, not a lifeguard. Wave data is coarse in small enclosed bays, and shelter is only known for hand-checked beaches. Always look at the water before you get in, and never leave a child unattended near the sea.
